## PT. 1 - ORIGINS

The Smoothie Bar story starts with high quality cannabis—frozen live, never dried or cured.
Immediately after harvest, each plant is manicured by hand, vacuum sealed, and cooled in stages, reaching as low as -40 degrees Celsius before extraction begins.

On the surface of every fresh frozen plant are trichomes—the resin glands where THC, terpenes, and hundreds of other active compounds live. Freezing protects those trichomes & keeps everything intact for extraction, which focuses on isolating the valuable compounds inside.

---

## PT. 2 - PRIDE IN PROCESS

Extraction uses a chilled butane/propane blend to pull THC, terpenes, and other compounds out of the plant material.

The frozen cannabis is ground and loaded into a mesh sleeve. The chilled solvent passes through, dissolving the valuable compounds and leaving the plant matter behind.

A skilled technician runs this closed-loop system from start to finish—controlling pressures, flow rates, and solvent contact to get a clean, complete extraction.

Once the oil is pulled, the solvent gets recovered and recycled for future runs. Nothing wasted.

What comes out is live resin—the plant's properties in a more potent and versatile form.

---

## PT. 3 - WHAT'S THE BLEND?

Every Smoothie Bar Blend vaporizer has three core components: cannabis distillate, live resin diamonds, and high-terpene extract.

**Distillate** is pure distilled cannabis oil. Also known as Delta-9 THC, at around 95% potency.

**Live resin diamonds** are THCa isolate in crystalline form. When heated, THCa converts into delta-9 THC through a reaction called decarboxylation. Delta-9 is the bioactive compound responsible for most of the psychoactive effects cannabis users experience.

Both of these extracts are extremely potent—but on their own, they're limited in flavor and aroma.

That's where **high-terpene extract (HTE)** comes in. This fraction of live resin is concentrated with terpenes and a wide range of similar active compounds. HTE is treated with minimal heat to preserve its natural essence—compounds that are valuable but don't last long under high temperatures.

HTE runs darker than distillate or diamonds, which is why the final formulations have an amber hue. The exact color shifts depending on each strain and its unique properties, and can even be affected by individual plants in a batch.

Finally, botanical terpenes—specifically, ones that otherwise naturally occur in cannabis—are selectively paired to complement each HTE flavor profile. That's how Smoothie Bar Blends achieve their complexity and full-bodied flavor. Each release has its own character, each collection achieving the ultimate balance. The formulation continues to evolve over time while staying rooted in the source material.

## PT. 4 - QUALITY CONTROL

The final steps are the most critical.

Every formulation is tested in-house, then sent to third-party labs to screen for contamination and quality control issues. All vaporizer hardware and packaging goes through quarantine, inspection, and testing before being cleared for use.

Tamper-proof packaging keeps the product secure on its journey to the consumer.

Every Smoothie Bar Blend that leaves the facility carries the full expression of the plant it came from—refined, tested, and ready to be experienced.
